<p>WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus travelled to the epicentre at the weekend and spoke to Congolese President Felix Tshisekedi, as health officials launched a public campaign to reassure the nation that the situation was under control. In neighbouring Kenya, violent protests erupted on Monday over a quarantine facility reserved for US patients arriving from the DRC. A 27-year-old man was shot dead during the clashes, according to rights group VOCAL Africa, while President William Ruto defended the facility as part of a national preparedness system.</p>
