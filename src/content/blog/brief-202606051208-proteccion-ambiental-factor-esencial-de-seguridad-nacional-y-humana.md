<p>Vietnamese President To Lam has warned that economic growth cannot be considered sustainable if citizens endure pollution and environmental degradation, calling environmental protection a “fundamental factor” of national and human security. The remarks came in an article published on Friday to mark World Environment Day and ahead of World Oceans Day on 8 June.</p>
