<p>A central element of the accord directly addresses the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow waterway critical to global energy flows. Under the terms, the United States will begin removing its naval blockade immediately and fully end it within 30 days, while Iran commits to facilitating safe commercial passage from the Persian Gulf to the Sea of Oman. Tehran will also initiate dialogue with Oman and other Gulf littoral states to define the future administration and maritime services of the strait in line with international law.</p>
