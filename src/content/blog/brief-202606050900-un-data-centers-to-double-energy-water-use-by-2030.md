<p>In a report released on June 3, the UN University Institute for Water, Environment and Health said annual data centre power use is projected to reach 945 terawatt-hours (TWh) by 2030, roughly equivalent to Japan’s entire electricity consumption, while water consumption will climb to 9.3 trillion litres. Last year, the sector used 448 TWh of electricity—more than Saudi Arabia—and 4.5 trillion litres of water, enough for over 600 million people in Sub-Saharan Africa, and emitted 189 million tonnes of carbon dioxide.</p>
