Khamenei, 86, was killed on February 28 when a U.S.-Israeli airstrike that launched the West Asia war struck his location. His flag-draped coffin, along with those of his relatives, was placed on a truck whose sides were styled to resemble the ornamental grating surrounding a revered shrine. Revolutionary Guard General Hasan Hasanzsdeh, who is overseeing the procession, said the coffins would be taken through the streets of Tehran on their way to Mehrabad International Airport over a 12-hour journey.
