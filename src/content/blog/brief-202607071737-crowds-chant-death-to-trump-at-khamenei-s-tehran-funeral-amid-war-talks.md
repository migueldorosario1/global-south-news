<p>Black-clad crowds packing central Tehran chanted calls for the death of U.S. President Donald Trump on Monday as the funeral procession for Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei began. Khamenei’s flag-draped coffin and those of his family members, killed in the February 28 U.S.-Israeli airstrike that triggered the war, were carried on a truck adorned to resemble the ornamental grating of a Shiite shrine.</p>
<p>The massive turnout, encouraged by Iran’s theocracy as a sign of strength, came as Tehran negotiates with Washington over a permanent end to the conflict. Revolutionary Guard Gen. Hasan Hasanzsdeh, overseeing the ceremonies, said the cortege would take 12 hours to travel through Tehran to Mehrabad International Airport.</p>
