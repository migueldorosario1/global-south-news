<p>One Indian sailor was killed and eight crew members injured when Iranian cruise missiles struck two UAE-flagged oil tankers in the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday, the United Arab Emirates Ministry of Defence said. The vessels, the Mombasa and Al Bahiyah, were in Omani territorial waters on the southern lane of the strategic waterway when they were hit. The ministry said the deceased was aboard the Mombasa, and six of the wounded were Indian nationals; the other two were Ukrainians. Four of the injured were in a serious condition.</p>
