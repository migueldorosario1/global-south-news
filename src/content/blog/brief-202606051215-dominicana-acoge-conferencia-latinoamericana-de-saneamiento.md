<p>Santo Domingo — Dominican health minister Víctor Atallah and Wellington Arnaud, executive director of the National Institute of Drinking Water and Sewerage (Inapa), on Tuesday inaugurated the Latin American Sanitation Conference (LatinoSan 2026) in the Dominican Republic, an event that frames sanitation as an essential human right and a cornerstone of public health and sustainable development.</p>
