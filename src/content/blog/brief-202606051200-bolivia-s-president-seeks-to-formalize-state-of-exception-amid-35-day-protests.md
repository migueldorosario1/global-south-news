<p>Bolivian President Paz submitted legislation to Congress this week to regulate the state of exception, a move that immediately drew opposition from within the government. The bill, announced on Wednesday as protests demanding Paz’s resignation entered their 35th day, aims to formalise the armed forces’ role in what the president described as a “humanitarian action” amid ongoing blockades.</p>
