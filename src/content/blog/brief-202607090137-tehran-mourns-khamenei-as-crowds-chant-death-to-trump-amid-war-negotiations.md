<p>Mourners dressed in black flooded into Tehran on Monday for a procession as part of the funeral of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, with crowds calling for the death of U.S. President Donald Trump. Khamenei’s flag-draped coffin, and those of his family members killed Feb. 28 in an airstrike at the start of the war launched by Israel and the United States, were placed on a truck decorated to resemble the ornamental grating around an imam’s shrine. The procession came as Iran negotiates with the U.S. over a permanent end to the war that killed the 86-year-old cleric. Revolutionary Guard Gen. Hasan Hasanzsdeh, overseeing the procession, said the coffins would be taken through Tehran to Mehrabad International Airport over a 12-hour journey.</p>
