<p>An Indian crew member was killed and eight others were wounded when Iranian cruise missiles struck two Emirati oil tankers in Omani territorial waters of the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday, the United Arab Emirates Ministry of Defence said. The attack on the Mombasa and Al Bahiyah marks the latest escalation in the strategic waterway.</p>
<p>The ministry said the tankers were targeted in the southern lane of the strait. The dead seafarer was aboard the Mombasa; six of the wounded were Indian nationals and two were Ukrainian. Four of the injured were listed in serious condition. Fires broke out on both tankers but were brought under control.</p>
<p>The UAE condemned what it called a “blatant attack” and asserted its full right to respond, while emphasising it remained prepared to deal with threats to national security. Iran offered no immediate comment.</p>
<p>The strike follows heightened tensions since the US and Israel launched military attacks on Iran in late February. On Monday, President Donald Trump announced the reimposition of a naval blockade on Iranian shipping, warning that US forces would hit Iran again “very hard” and demanding fees for safe passage. Iran’s top joint military command rejected any US role in the waterway.</p>
<p>Before the conflict, around a fifth of the world’s oil and gas traffic passed through Hormuz daily, delivering more than 15 million barrels of fuel to global markets.</p>
