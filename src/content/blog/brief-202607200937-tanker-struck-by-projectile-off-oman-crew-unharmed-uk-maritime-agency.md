<p>A separate report from the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) on Tuesday said a tanker was hit by an unknown projectile 40 nautical miles northeast of Oman’s Qalhat, piercing the starboard engine room. The crew were safe. Reuters could not immediately verify whether the UKMTO report referred to the same incident as the one reported by the UAE.</p>
