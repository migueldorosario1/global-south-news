<p>Under the MOU, the United States will immediately begin removal of its naval blockade, fully lifting it within 30 days, and will withdraw forces from near Iran within 30 days after a final agreement. Iran will facilitate safe passage for commercial vessels through the Strait of Hormuz without charge for 60 days, conduct de-mining, and engage Oman and other Gulf littoral states on future maritime administration. The document also pledges a US-led $300 billion reconstruction and economic development plan for Iran, with regional partners, and all necessary financial licenses are to be granted.</p>
