<p>Hundreds of thousands of Iranians converged on Tehran on Monday for the funeral procession of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, with crowds chanting for the death of U.S. President Donald Trump. Khamenei’s flag-draped coffin, and those of his family killed in an airstrike on February 28 at the start of the war launched by Israel and the United States, were placed on a truck. Authorities decorated the truck’s side to resemble the ornamental grating that surrounds the shrine of an imam. The massive turnout, encouraged by Iran’s theocracy as a sign of strength, came as it negotiates with the U.S. over a permanent end to the war that killed the 86-year-old cleric. Revolutionary Guard Gen. Hasan Hasanzsdeh, who is overseeing the procession, said the coffins would be taken through the streets of Tehran on their way to Mehrabad International Airport over a 12-hour journey. Meanwhile, President Trump said there would either be a deal with Iran or the U.S. would “finish the job.”</p>
