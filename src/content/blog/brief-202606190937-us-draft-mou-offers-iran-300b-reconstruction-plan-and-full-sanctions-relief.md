<p>A central incentive is a at least $300 billion reconstruction and economic development plan for Iran, to be designed with regional partners and financed through mechanisms finalized within 60 days. The U.S. pledges to terminate all unilateral and UN Security Council sanctions on an agreed schedule, and immediately issue waivers for Iranian oil exports, including associated banking, insurance, and transport services. Frozen Iranian assets would be fully released for use under Central Bank of Iran designation.</p>
