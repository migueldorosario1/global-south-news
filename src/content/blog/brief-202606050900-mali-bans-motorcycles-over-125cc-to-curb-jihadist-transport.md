<p>Mali’s military government has banned the use of motorcycles with engine capacities of 125cc and above outside major urban centres, a move aimed at hobbling jihadist groups that rely on them for transport and attacks. The decree, read on state television on Wednesday (June 3), also imposes a nationwide suspension of the import, transit, marketing, sale and free distribution of such vehicles and their accessories.</p>
