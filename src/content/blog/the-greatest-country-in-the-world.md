<p>In an article published on his official website <a href="http://www.nogueirabatista.com.br" target="_blank" rel="noopener">www.nogueirabatista.com.br</a>, economist Paulo Nogueira Batista Jr. expresses his growing admiration for the Islamic Republic of Iran. He questions which other country in the world faces the United States and Israel practically alone and so far with great success.</p>

<p>Iran has taken immense risks to defend other nations in the region from foreign aggression. This courageous stance was demonstrated when the Iranian military retaliated against Israel for the barbarities committed in Lebanon.</p>

<p>Iran is deservedly the nation that arouses the most widespread admiration in the entire world nowadays. This sentiment is growing globally, except obviously within the political establishments of Israel and the United States.</p>

<p>Even inside the United States, public opinion seems to be tilting gradually toward Iran's perspective. At the same time, domestic support for the Trump administration and the Israeli regime is falling.</p>

<p>The powerful Zionist lobby has bought off most of the political class in the United States. Through this influence, it has succeeded in subordinating the actions of the superpower to Israel's aggressive priorities.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, the corporate media does its best to defend Israeli actions at all costs. These outlets manipulate and conceal critical information in a systematic and deliberate way.</p>

<p>However, Israel has gone too far in its military campaigns and lost all international respect. The country has become the most widely hated nation across the globe.</p>

<p>The American people, for their part, are finally realizing that unconditional support for Israel is proving very expensive. The perception is growing among voters that the United States is fighting a war that does not interest them.</p>

<p>It has become entirely evident that the ongoing war in West Asia is primarily in the Israeli interest. This situation represents a classic case of the tail wagging the dog.</p>

<p>A small country of about ten million inhabitants calls the shots for a global superpower. Fortunately, support for Israel is finally reaching the most sensitive part of the human body, which is the pocket.</p>

<p>As a direct result of the conflict, the prices of gasoline, food, and other essential products have risen sharply. This economic pain threatens to defeat the Republicans in the midterm congressional elections in November.</p>

<p>The main reason that the economic impact is not worse is that Western countries are running down their strategic oil reserves. These reserves are not unlimited and are apparently about to run out completely.</p>

<p>It is no wonder that US President Donald Trump accepted a truce that amounts to a recognition of defeat. He seems eager to find a reasonably honorable way to end the conflict into which he was dragged.</p>

<p>Most countries have been heavily affected by the rising prices of oil, petrochemicals, fertilizers, and food. The blame for this crisis lies not with Iran, which is acting in legitimate self-defense.</p>

<p>The responsibility belongs to the aggressors, namely the United States and Israel, who threw West Asia into a disastrous situation. Only a few major oil exporters have benefited from this global turmoil so far.</p>

<p>These exporting countries, including Brazil, do not depend on the Strait of Hormuz and are selling more oil at higher prices. However, even these nations are feeling the impact of global inflation on their domestic economies.</p>

<p>Insecurity about the future remains enormous, and the full extent of the global damage is still unknown. But one thing is certain: the war is resulting in a weakening of the American Empire and Israel.</p>

<p>The aggressors are finally paying a heavy price for their countless international crimes. In retrospect, the war against Iran will likely be seen as a milestone in the decline of the United States.</p>

<p>This historic transition will accelerate the consolidation of a multipolar world. The best and noblest countries deserve all our respect and gratitude in this struggle.</p>
