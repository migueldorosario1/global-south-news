<p>The United States and Iran have released the text of a joint memorandum of understanding that declares an immediate and permanent ceasefire on all current war fronts, including in Lebanon, and commits both sides to negotiating a final comprehensive deal within 60 days. The undated document, published by The Hindu on Thursday, was not signed by named officials but outlines sweeping reciprocal steps to end hostilities, lift economic pressure and resolve nuclear tensions.</p>
