<p>The agreement, outlined in a joint statement following Sharif’s meeting with Xi and Premier Li Qiang, signals a renewed push to revitalise the flagship Belt and Road Initiative project after years of delays and security challenges. The two sides agreed to upgrade the Karakoram Highway, the vital overland artery linking western China to Pakistan, and enhance road and port infrastructure around the Khunjerab Pass. Crucially, they extended an invitation for third-party participation in CPEC projects, opening the corridor to multilateral investment under the model agreed.</p>
