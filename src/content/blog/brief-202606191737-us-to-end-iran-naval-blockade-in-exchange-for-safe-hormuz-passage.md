Under the terms, the US must begin removing its naval blockade against Iran upon signature and fully end it within 30 days. The US further undertakes to withdraw its forces from areas near Iranian territory within 30 days after a final deal is reached. In return, Iran is to facilitate safe passage for commercial vessels through the Strait of Hormuz within 30 days, engaging with Oman and other Gulf states on future maritime administration.
