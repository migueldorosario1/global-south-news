<p>Under the MOU, Washington agrees to lift its naval blockade fully within 30 days, withdraw forces from near Iran after a final deal, and terminate all sanctions—including UN Security Council and IAEA measures—on an agreed schedule. It also pledges at least $300 billion for Iran’s reconstruction and economic development, and to release all frozen Iranian assets. In return, Tehran will arrange safe passage for commercial vessels through the Strait of Hormuz, maintain the status quo of its nuclear programme, and reaffirm it will not develop nuclear weapons. The MOU establishes that enriched uranium will be down-blended on site under IAEA oversight as the minimum methodology, with a mutually agreed framework to address broader enrichment and nuclear needs.</p>
