<p>Moscow will convene the international Primakov Readings forum, bringing together senior officials and experts from at least 20 countries, including Yuri Ushakov, assistant to the president of Russia, and Sergei Lavrov, Russia’s foreign minister. Organised by the Institute of World Economy and International Relations (IMEMO) and the E.M. Primakov Center for Foreign Policy Cooperation, the gathering will proceed under the banner “A world without rules: a game of power?”</p>
