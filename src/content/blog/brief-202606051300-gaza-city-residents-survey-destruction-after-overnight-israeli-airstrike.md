<p>Debris covered nearby streets and several sections of a residential building were damaged after an overnight Israeli airstrike in Gaza City. Witnesses described scenes of panic after the strike, which occurred around 2 a.m., as emergency crews responded to reports of fire and casualties. Residents spent the morning clearing rubble and searching through damaged belongings while children and families remained gathered near the site. The strike comes as fighting continues across Gaza despite discussions about ending the conflict. Relatives said the attack destroyed homes, personal possessions and livelihoods. Residents questioned how talk of a potential end to the war could continue while airstrikes remain a daily reality. The incident highlights the ongoing humanitarian impact of the conflict, with residential areas bearing the consequences of military operations and repeated displacement.</p>
