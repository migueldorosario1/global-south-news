<p>Under the MOU, Washington commits to lifting its naval blockade of Iran within 30 days and to withdrawing forces from near Iranian territory 30 days after the final deal. Tehran pledges to ensure safe passage for commercial vessels through the Strait of Hormuz, with traffic starting immediately and fully instated within 30 days following de-mining and removal of technical obstacles. The two sides also undertake to respect each other’s sovereignty and refrain from interference in internal affairs.</p>
