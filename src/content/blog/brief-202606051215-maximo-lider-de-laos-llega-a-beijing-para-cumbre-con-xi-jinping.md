<p>President Thongloun Sisoulith of Laos arrived in Beijing for a summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping, marking the 65th anniversary of diplomatic relations between the two socialist states. The visit, which began in Hangzhou on June 2 and runs through Saturday, underscores a deepening partnership anchored by shared communist party governance and expanding economic ties.</p>
<p>Central to the agenda is the fifth anniversary of the China-Laos railway, a flagship project under Beijing’s Belt and Road Initiative. Since commencing operations in December 2021, the rail link has facilitated cumulative trade valued at over 80 billion yuan ($11.1 billion), according to data from the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade. In the first quarter of 2026 alone, imports and exports transported via the line reached 6.81 billion yuan ($948 million), a year-on-year surge of 62.7 percent.</p>
<p>The Central Committee of the Communist Party of China recently reaffirmed the strategic importance Beijing places on bilateral relations with Vientiane. The committee noted that both countries are led by communist parties and expressed readiness to strengthen strategic communication and advance the construction of a shared future. Laos and China have maintained formal diplomatic ties since 1961 and continue to develop a comprehensive strategic partnership, with active cooperation across infrastructure, trade, and regional connectivity.</p>
