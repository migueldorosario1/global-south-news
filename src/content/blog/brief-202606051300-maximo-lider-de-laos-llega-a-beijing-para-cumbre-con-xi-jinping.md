<p>Laos President Thongloun Sisoulith arrived in Beijing on Thursday for talks with Chinese President Xi Jinping, a visit that marks the 65th anniversary of diplomatic relations and celebrates the fifth year of operations of the China-Laos railway, a signature project under Beijing’s Belt and Road Initiative.</p>
<p>Sisoulith, who opened his trip in Hangzhou on June 2, is expected to discuss strengthening cooperation between two socialist states governed by communist parties. The Central Committee of the Communist Party of China recently reaffirmed that Beijing attaches strategic importance to bilateral ties and is willing to deepen strategic communication, expand exchanges and advance a shared-future community.</p>
<p>Trade data underscores the railway’s growing role in regional commerce. According to the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade, the value of goods transported on the line hit 6.81 billion yuan ($948 million) in the first quarter of 2026, a year-on-year surge of 62.7 percent. Since operations began in December 2021, cumulative trade carried by the railway has exceeded 80 billion yuan ($11.1 billion).</p>
<p>China and Laos established diplomatic relations in 1961 and have built a comprehensive strategic partnership anchored by cooperation in infrastructure, trade and cross-border connectivity. The railway, linking the Chinese city of Kunming to Vientiane, has emerged as a prominent symbol of that collaboration, cutting transit times and boosting commerce across the Mekong region.</p>
<p>Sisoulith’s visit, which runs through Saturday, is likely to yield new agreements that further integrate Laos into China’s regional infrastructure network while reinforcing political alignment between the two ruling parties in Southeast Asia.</p>
