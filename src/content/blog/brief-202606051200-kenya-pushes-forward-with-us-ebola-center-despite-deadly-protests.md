<p>The centre, located about 200km from Nairobi, was due to open last week with 50 isolation beds managed by US medical staff but was temporarily halted by a court order. Public anger erupted on Monday, with protests that turned deadly, according to a rights group. Critics view the US-run facility on Kenyan soil as a violation of sovereignty with colonial undertones, while also fearing the risk of Ebola spreading in the country.</p>
