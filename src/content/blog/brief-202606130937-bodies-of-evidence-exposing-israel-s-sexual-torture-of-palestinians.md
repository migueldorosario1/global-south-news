<p>Abdel Latif Ghaith, director of the prisoner rights group Addameer, recalled witnessing the 1969 interrogation of Rasmea Odeh, who was stripped naked and severely tortured. He described seeing her naked in the interrogation room and hearing the interrogation from an adjacent cell. In later UN testimony, Odeh said she was raped with a stick, given electric shocks to her genitals and mouth, and threatened that her father would be forced to rape her.</p><p>Kifaya Khraim, international advocacy coordinator at the Women’s Centre for Legal Aid and Counselling, said Israeli forces exploit the social stigma around sexual violence to inflict harm, knowing that many women are unable to identify what they undergo as rape.</p>
