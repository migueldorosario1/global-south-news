<p>The communiqué further stressed real-time sharing of epidemiological data to enable early case detection and a coordinated regional response. Ten mobile laboratories are currently deployed across the area—including in the DRC, Uganda, Kenya, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan and Tanzania—to facilitate rapid screening of suspected Ebola cases. Ministers directed the secretariat to maintain this field capacity at strategic border points and high-risk zones while mobilising additional financial and technical resources for surveillance activities.</p>
